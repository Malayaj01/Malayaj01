## currently building

### 🚀 [Switchkrr](https://github.com/Malayaj01/Switchkrrr) — mentor-led job switching

Most people job-hunt alone, with no direction, no referrals and no accountability. Switchkrr turns it into a guided workflow between a candidate and a mentor who already works where they want to go.

A candidate builds a profile, browses **anonymised** mentor previews, and sends a request. When a mentor approves, a private workspace called a **Hustle** opens — the mentor posts leads and guidance, the candidate tracks applications from lead to offer, and both sides see the full progress history.

Three role-aware dashboards, mentor verification with a real audit trail, and enforced capacity limits so mentors never get overloaded.

`Next.js 16` `TypeScript` `Prisma` `PostgreSQL` `Docker`

> *Chal aaja, switch krte hai.*
